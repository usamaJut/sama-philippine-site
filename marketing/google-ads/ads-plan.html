What you do, step by step

  1. Google Business Profile (free, do this first). At business.google.com, find your listing and claim it. Add the website philippines.samatravell.com, your WhatsApp number, and the services "تجديد العقود" and "جوب أوردر". Add your logo and photos.
  2. Google Search Console (free). At search.google.com/search-console, add the property https://philippines.samatravell.com/ and verify it. Then open Sitemaps and submit sitemap.xml. Send me the verification code if it asks for a tag.
  3. Google Ads account. At ads.google.com, create the account and choose Expert mode. Enter the settings, keywords, and texts above. You enter the payment details yourself.
  4. Conversion tracking. Once the account exists, send me your Google tag ID (it starts with AW-). I'll add tracking so Google counts WhatsApp button clicks.
  5. Review after a week. Look at the search terms report. Add any irrelevant words to the negative list.
